Provenance & Interpretation

How the dashboard uses the cleaned project dataset

  • All KPIs, charts, heatmaps, distributions and relationships are calculated from the cleaned CSV.
  • Missing source values are excluded from the relevant average or correlation; they are never treated as zero.
  • The cleaned file contains 4,000 records and 28 variables. Yield gaps are reconstructed in the processed dataset.
  • Filters apply to every calculated section and chart.
  • Correlation indicates association and does not establish causation.
